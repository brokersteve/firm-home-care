Emergency?

Fire, gas smell or crime: call 911 first. Then, or for flooding, no heat in freezing weather or anything unsafe, call our emergency maintenance line.

Call 704-430-6193

Water leaking?

Turn the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet clockwise. If water is spraying or spreading, shut off the main water to the home, then call.

Power out in part of the home?

Check the breaker panel for a switch in the middle or OFF position. Push it fully OFF, then back ON. For a dead outlet in a kitchen, bath, garage or outside, press RESET on the nearest outlet with buttons.
